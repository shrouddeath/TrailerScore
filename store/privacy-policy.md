# TrailerScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

TrailerScore is a browser extension that shows movie ratings (IMDb, Letterboxd, Rotten Tomatoes) under trailers on YouTube.

## The short version

TrailerScore does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server operated by us.

## What the extension does on your device

- **Reads the video title** on `youtube.com` watch pages to recognise which trailer you are watching.
- **Loads public rating pages** from `imdb.com`, `letterboxd.com` and `rottentomatoes.com` directly from your browser to read the scores. These requests go straight from your browser to those sites, just like opening the pages yourself. Requests to imdb.com include the cookies your browser already holds for imdb.com, because IMDb only serves its pages to browsers that have visited it before.
- **Stores locally**: your settings (which sources to show, display style), a short list of recently checked trailers with their scores, and a cache of fetched scores for up to 12 hours. This data lives in Chrome's extension storage on your device. Settings may be synced between your own devices by Chrome if you have Chrome sync turned on.
- **Optional OMDb key**: if you enter an OMDb API key in the settings, it is stored locally and sent only to `omdbapi.com` with your requests, as a fallback for the IMDb rating. The key is never sent anywhere else.

You can clear the history and the cache at any time in the extension's settings. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its data.

## Third-party sites

When you click a chip, or when the extension loads a rating page in the background, the privacy policies of IMDb, Letterboxd, Rotten Tomatoes, OMDb and YouTube apply to those requests. TrailerScore is not affiliated with any of them.

## Permissions explained

- `storage`: save settings, history and cache on your device.
- Access to `youtube.com`: read the video title and show the score bar.
- Access to `imdb.com`, `letterboxd.com`, `rottentomatoes.com`: read the scores from their public pages.
- Access to `omdbapi.com`: optional fallback, only used with your own API key.
